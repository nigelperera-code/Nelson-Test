FAQ

Common questions

What size of business do you target?

Typically owner-operated companies with meaningful profitability. Our initial focus is ~£250k–£500k profit, with flexibility for the right fit.

Do you require sellers to stay on?

Not always. We’re flexible: full exit, phased transition, or partial rollover — depending on what’s best for the business.

How quickly can you move?

If there’s a fit and information is available, we can move fast. We prioritise clarity and momentum over endless back-and-forth.

Will you keep the brand and team?

Usually, yes. We prefer continuity. The goal is to protect what works, then improve what’s holding the business back.
